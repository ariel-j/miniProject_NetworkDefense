Currently Under Attack?

If you suspect you're currently experiencing a cyber attack, follow the Immediate Response steps below right now.

🎯 Why Incident Response Matters

Even security-conscious individuals can fall victim to sophisticated phishing attacks. The key is responding quickly and effectively to minimize damage.

⏰ Critical Timeline

  • 62 minutes - Average time security teams have to contain a breach
  • 2% - Percentage of organizations that meet their 24-hour recovery target
  • $4.45M - Average cost of a data breach in 2023
  • 287 days - Average time to identify and contain a breach

Quick response can mean the difference between a minor incident and a major security breach. This guide provides step-by-step instructions for immediate response, assessment, and recovery.

URGENT - Security Incident Report From: [Your Name] Date: [Current Date/Time] Subject: Potential Phishing Attack - Immediate Response Required I am reporting a potential security incident: What happened: [Brief description of what you clicked/downloaded] When: [Date and time of incident] Systems affected: [Your computer/accounts] Current status: [Computer disconnected from network] Immediate actions taken: [List what you've done] I am available at [phone number].
