🎯 Why Incident Response Matters

Even security-conscious individuals can fall victim to sophisticated phishing attacks. The key is responding quickly and effectively to minimize damage. With cybersecurity teams having just 62 minutes on average to prevent a breach after initial compromise, swift action is critical.

Response Statistics:

  • 62 minutes: Average time to contain a breach after detection (down from 79 minutes in 2022)
  • 98% recovery goal: Organizations aim to recover within 24 hours
  • 2% actual success: Only 2% achieve their target recovery time
  • $4.45 million: Average cost of a data breach in 2024

Having a structured response plan can reduce recovery time by up to 77% and significantly limit financial and reputational damage.
