🎯 What is Login Form Spoofing?

Login form spoofing is a technique where attackers create fake login pages that look identical to legitimate websites. When users enter their credentials, the information is captured and sent to the attacker instead of the real service.

How It Works:

  • Visual Mimicry: Perfect recreation of legitimate login interfaces
  • URL Deception: Similar-looking domains or subdomains
  • SSL Certificate Abuse: Free certificates to show "secure" padlock
  • Form Harvesting: Capturing and storing entered credentials

These attacks are particularly dangerous because they look completely legitimate to users, making detection extremely difficult without proper training.

🔬 Detection Techniques

URL Analysis:

  • Domain Verification: Always check the exact domain name
  • Character Substitution: Look for 0 vs O, rn vs m, etc.
  • Subdomain Tricks: Watch for accounts.fake-site.com vs accounts.google.com
  • TLD Variations: .com vs .co, .net, .org variations

Visual Inspection:

  • Logo Quality: Pixelated or slightly off logos
  • Color Schemes: Subtle color differences
  • Typography: Different fonts or spacing
  • Layout Issues: Misaligned elements or poor responsive design

Technical Verification:

  • SSL Certificate: Check certificate details and issuer
  • Page Source: Look for suspicious scripts or external links
  • Form Action: Verify where the form data is being sent
  • Browser Warnings: Pay attention to security warnings
